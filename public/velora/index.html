07   /   Before your evening

A few
thoughtful details.

A little preparation, so you can simply arrive and enjoy. These are example policies for this fictional restaurant.

What is the dress code?

Our sample dress code is smart casual. Dress for an evening that feels special to you; jackets are welcome but not required.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Share allergies and dietary requirements when requesting a reservation. A real restaurant’s team must confirm what it can safely accommodate; the vegetarian menu filter is not an allergy-safety guarantee. No accommodation is confirmed through this demo.

How far ahead can I reserve?

This mockup lets you explore dates within the next 90 days. All times are simulated, and submitting the form creates a preview only. Parties of seven or more are guided to the private-dining inquiry.

How long is the tasting experience?

Allow approximately two and a half hours for the illustrative seven-course experience. Wine and zero-proof pairings are optional. Sample prices exclude tax and gratuity.

What is the cancellation policy?

The example policy is to request changes at least 24 hours before dining. A production reservation system would display the restaurant’s actual policy, any deposit, and applicable cancellation charges before confirmation. This demo never charges a card.

What about parking and accessibility?

Velora is a fictional Evansville concept, so its sample address is not an operating venue and has no verified entrance, parking, or accessibility information. A real site would publish confirmed arrival details and a direct contact for access questions here.
