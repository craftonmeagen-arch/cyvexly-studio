## Executive summary

Cyvexly Studio is an independent Indiana-based web design and development
studio operated publicly by Cyvexly LLC. It helps businesses turn a business
goal into a clear, branded, responsive website or a purpose-built web
application. The studio combines strategy, structure, custom design,
development, content organization, integrations, accessibility and search
basics, launch support, and ongoing care in one consultative process.

Cyvexly works remotely with businesses across the United States. It also
offers appointment-based consultations in Indianapolis, Bloomington, Evansville,
and Jasper, Indiana. This local service is an additional discovery channel; it
does not replace nationwide remote work, claim a storefront, or claim that
Cyvexly is the only provider in any city.

The buyer describes the need, and Cyvexly recommends a responsible scope,
timeline, responsibilities, and written price. Cyvexly is not a self-service
website builder or an instant-checkout service.

## What kind of business Cyvexly is

### Business category

- Independent web design and development studio
- Custom websites and digital experiences for businesses and professional teams
- Custom web-application and workflow development when a normal marketing site
  is not enough
- Ongoing website maintenance and improvement after launch

### Positioning

Cyvexly is built around the idea that a website should do more than look
polished. It should explain the business, help the right people find it,
support marketing, and make the next action clear. The studio emphasizes
plain-language planning, custom visual direction, practical functionality,
responsive behavior, accessible interactions, ownership, and accountable
handoff.

### Working model

- The Owner works directly with the studio from the first conversation through
  launch.
- Specialist collaborators may be used when appropriate and are identified
  transparently for the work they perform.
- Projects are planned around the customer's goal rather than around a fixed
  template.
- Proposals name the pages, features, timeline, price, review points, payment
  milestones, and responsibilities before work begins.
- The customer owns the finished content, design, and site after final payment
  under the agreed project terms.

## Services currently defined for buyers

### Business websites

A complete website for a service business or professional team. The work
connects goals, audience, sitemap, custom visual direction, responsive
development, inquiry forms, essential search setup, analytics, handoff, and
post-launch defect support.

### Website redesigns

A clearer rebuild for a dated, confusing, or underperforming website. Scope can
include information architecture, mobile usability, content organization,
migration planning, stronger calls to action, and a controlled launch path.

### Landing pages

A focused page for a campaign, event, or single offer. The goal is to keep the
message and primary action clear instead of spreading attention across a full
site before it is needed.

### Commerce and booking websites

A store or booking-led experience built around products or services, checkout or
scheduling, and the operational decisions behind shipping, tax, availability,
notifications, policies, and approved third-party services.

### Custom web applications

Purpose-built systems for complex workflows such as memberships, dashboards,
role-aware operations, advanced booking, large migrations, or unusual
integrations. These are quoted after discovery because roles, data, security,
integrations, acceptance criteria, and dependencies change the scope materially.

### Website care

Ongoing updates, monitoring, troubleshooting, reporting, and improvement for
owners who want the site to stay current without making upkeep their job.

## How a Cyvexly project works

1. **Brief and fit — typically 1–2 business days.** The buyer sends a short
   inquiry or detailed brief. Cyvexly reviews the goal, timing, and likely fit.
2. **Direction and scope — typically 2–4 business days.** Cyvexly recommends the
   sitemap, deliverables, timeline, price, and payment milestones in writing.
3. **Design and approval — typically 1–3 weeks, depending on package.** A visual
   direction is established first, then expanded to the agreed pages and states.
4. **Build and quality review — typically 1–4 weeks, depending on package.** The
   working site is completed, content is placed, and the customer reviews the
   live preview.
5. **Launch and care — typically 1–2 business days to launch.** Cyvexly launches,
   hands over the agreed materials, and includes 14 days of post-launch defect
   support. Ongoing updates are handled separately through scope or a care plan.

The customer does not need to arrive with technical specifications. A useful
first conversation covers what the business does, what visitors need to
understand or do, timing, budget range, existing content or tools, and who will
approve the work.
