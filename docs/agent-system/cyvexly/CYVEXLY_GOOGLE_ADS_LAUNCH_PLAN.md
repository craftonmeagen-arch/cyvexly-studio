# Cyvexly Google Search advertising launch plan

**Prepared:** September 14, 2026
**Status:** Account and billing setup exist. The Search campaign is fully built
and ready to publish, but it remains an inactive draft and has not begun spend.
**Public brand:** Cyvexly Studio
**Goal:** Qualified website-project inquiries, not traffic for its own sake.

This is an owner-only operating record. It contains no passwords, payment
details, advertising identifiers, or provider secrets.

## Launch boundary

- Start with one Google **Search** campaign. Do not enable Display, YouTube,
  Demand Gen, Performance Max, or broad nationwide advertising in the first
  test.
- Initial locations: Indianapolis, Bloomington, Evansville, and Jasper,
  Indiana. Use the location option **Presence: people in or regularly in the
  targeted locations** to reduce clicks from people merely interested in a
  city.
- Preserve truthful nationwide remote availability on the website. Local ad
  targeting is a budget choice, not an exclusive service boundary or a claim
  of a local office.
- Owner-approved launch control: **$2 average per day** with a **$2 maximum CPC
  bid limit**. At that unchanged daily average, Google's normal monthly
  charging limit is approximately **$60.80** (2 × 30.4), and an individual day
  may serve up to **$4**. Publishing still requires explicit Owner confirmation
  at the final action because it can begin charges.
- Do not accept a promotional-credit offer merely because it is displayed.
  Evaluate its required spend and terms against the chosen test budget.
- Billing setup has been completed. The Owner must still explicitly confirm the
  final action that publishes the enabled campaign.

## Measurement prerequisite

The production site already loads GA4 behind visitor consent and records a
success-only `generate_lead` event for Contact, Planner, and Consultation. No
form contents or personal details are sent with that event.

Completed before activation on September 14, 2026:

1. The correct Cyvexly GA4 property is linked to Google Ads; GA4 audience
   importing remains off.
2. `generate_lead` is registered as a GA4 key event and imported as the primary
   **Submit lead form** conversion.
3. The imported conversion counts **One** conversion per ad interaction and has
   no invented monetary value.
4. Phone calls, page views, outbound clicks, and other superficial activity are
   not selected as campaign conversions.
5. The live site continues to send the success-only event only after visitor
   analytics consent. GA4 may take up to 24 hours to surface first event data,
   and Google Ads currently reports the new action as awaiting conversions.

## Initial network and bidding controls

- Google Search only for the first test; exclude Display and initially disable
  Search Partners so search-term quality is easier to interpret.
- English language.
- Use a controlled click-based launch strategy until the account has trustworthy
  lead data, then evaluate conversion-based bidding. Do not optimize toward page
  views.
- Do not launch automated recommendations that expand locations, keywords,
  networks, or budget without review.

## Decision rules after launch

- Check spend, search terms, location matches, and conversion tracking within
  the first two business days; correct obvious waste immediately.
- Review qualified inquiries weekly. A form submission is not automatically a
  qualified lead.
- At 30 days, report spend, clicks, qualified inquiries, cost per qualified
  inquiry, city/ad-group performance, and observed search terms.
- Pause or restructure if tracking is broken, irrelevant searches dominate, or
  the budget cannot buy enough qualified traffic to evaluate honestly.
- Do not claim advertising improved organic rankings. Paid and organic search
  are measured separately.
