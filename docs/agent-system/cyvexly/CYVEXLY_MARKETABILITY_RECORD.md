### 4. Google search discovery and measurement

- Public indexing was enabled and verified on September 12, 2026.
- The Google Search Console **domain property** for `cyvexly.com` is verified.
- Google accepted and successfully processed
  <https://cyvexly.com/sitemap.xml>. It reported 22 discovered pages at the
