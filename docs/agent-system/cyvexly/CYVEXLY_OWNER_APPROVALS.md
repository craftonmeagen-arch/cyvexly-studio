## Privacy business approach — 2026-09-10

**Status:** APPROVED IN PRINCIPLE — baseline practices, with the refinements
below required before treating the Privacy notice as ready for publication.
**Source:** Owner accepts the assistant's recommendation if appropriate for
a small startup and delegates judgment on these privacy practices.

### Assessment and accepted approach

This is a proportionate baseline for Cyvexly's inquiry-led business, not a
universal industry-standard certification or legal-compliance opinion:

- Collect the contact/project details needed to reply and prepare proposals.
- Use Resend to deliver submitted inquiries to the business inbox; keep
  unfinished Planner drafts local to the visitor's browser.
- Preserve proportionate spam protection, including IP-based rate limiting.
- Do not sell personal information or send unrequested marketing.
- Provide access/correction/deletion requests through design@cyvexly.com,
  with appropriate identity checks and legitimate retention exceptions.

### Required primary Builder follow-up

1. Minimize IP retention: remove routine raw-IP inclusion from both Contact
   and Planner internal email text/HTML while preserving rate limiting and
   abuse defenses. The current source includes these IPs; this is a correction
   to the assistant's earlier recommendation, not an already-deployed change.
   Do not add new personal-data logging. Test with synthetic submissions.
2. Document an operational retention schedule, separating inactive inquiries
   from client, accounting, and dispute records. A review of inactive leads
   after 12 months is a proposed business default, not a statutory period or
   an approved automatic-deletion promise. Do not delete existing records or
   claim a schedule is operational without verifying the actual process.
3. Make Privacy accurately distinguish no application submission database
   from provider storage. Resend can store message content/metadata, inboxes
   retain mail, and infrastructure may retain technical logs. Do not claim
   submissions exist only in successfully delivered emails or that deletion
   from one inbox erases all provider copies. Verify account-specific behavior
   before publishing exact provider retention periods.
4. Reconcile the notice with all actual form fields and tested behavior.
   Keep collection, sharing, local drafts, retention, and requests understandable.
   Update policy and implementation together; do not state future fixes as
   present facts. Check restricted mailbox/provider access and MFA separately
   without assuming those controls are already enabled.

### Sources checked September 10, 2026

- [FTC: Protecting Personal Information](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business)
  supports collecting only needed data, limiting access, and written retention
  and disposal policies. The IP-email refinement is our application of that
  guidance to Cyvexly, not an FTC rule specifically about inquiry emails.
- [Resend: message-content storage](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/how-do-i-ensure-sensitive-data-isnt-stored-on-resend)
  documents an optional storage-disabling feature; do not assume it is enabled
  for this account or purchase/change a plan under this direction.
